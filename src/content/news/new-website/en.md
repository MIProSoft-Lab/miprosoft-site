We have launched a new website for the group. It brings together:

- **People**: a profile for each member, with their publications, education and other activity.
- **Publications**: every paper by the group, once each, with a filter by author, citation figures and open-access links.
- **News and blog**: announcements and posts about what we do, like this one.

Publications are kept up to date automatically from [ORCID](https://orcid.org), so the site stays current without manual editing.
